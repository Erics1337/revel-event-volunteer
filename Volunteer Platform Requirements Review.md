**Volunteer Platform Requirements Review**

-   Onboarding experience for new volunteers
    -   Split button: “Add availability” or “See available shifts”
    -   Remove KPIs from volunteer dashboard - volunteers don’t care about these metrics
-   Shift browsing and filtering system
    -   Filter by days of week and locations
    -   Show role expectations clearly on each shift (room runner, registration, etc.)
    -   Include information bubbles explaining what each role entails
        -   Room runner: setting up chairs, banners, room preparation
        -   Registration: greeting people, distributing lanyards
        -   Door monitor: crowd control, directing attendees
        -   Building runner: general facility management
    -   Progress bar showing volunteer capacity per shift
    -   Allow volunteers to see who else is signed up (with privacy disclosure)
-   Shift structure changes
    -   Move from 4-hour to 2-hour shifts for better volunteer participation
    -   Previous feedback: 4+ hours too difficult for people to commit
    -   Shorter shifts allow volunteers to attend sessions at their assigned locations

**User Experience Improvements**

-   Volunteer dashboard navigation
    -   Top nav instead of left nav for better UX
    -   “My Schedule” tab with cancel functionality (not sign-up)
    -   “My Availability” tab for editing availability windows
    -   “My Profile” section for dietary needs, preferences
-   Shift sign-up flow
    -   Detail modal first before confirmation
    -   Inline confirmation with button changing to “Cancel”
    -   Logic: only surface shifts within volunteer’s availability windows
    -   Exception: option to view shifts outside set availability
-   Admin functionality
    -   Send targeted recruitment messages for understaffed shifts/days
    -   Automated day-before reminders to assigned volunteers
    -   Tabular volunteer view with names, emails, contact info
    -   Remove skills column - not needed for this use case
